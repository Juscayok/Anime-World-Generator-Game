## Artwork registration

The atlases contain transparent alpha. Their generated rows are not uniformly spaced, so battle-art.js records measured source bounds for each named character and pose. Row-major entries are explicit in battle.js. The top half supplies standing poses and the bottom half defeated poses. All 93 wheel opponents are represented; group entries use group illustrations. Artwork is illustrative fan art rather than a canonical power/form model. Player bodies remain a modular humanoid interpretation of the build. Female age heads, existing outfits, equipment, power effects, and pose pieces are combined locally. No runtime AI calls or credentials are needed.

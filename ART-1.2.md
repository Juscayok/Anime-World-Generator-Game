## Integration

The portrait atlas has true alpha. Its generated row heights differ, so portrait.js uses measured source rectangles. Four ages, nine outfits and three raster equipment motifs are composed with local aura/marking effects. The renderer derives appearance from saved results, including a preview of a pending roll. It does not generate unique anatomy or a bespoke drawing of every ability. Gender and facial customization are not part of the current wheel data. Statistics remain in the sheet. Reaction sheets use measured row boundaries where needed.

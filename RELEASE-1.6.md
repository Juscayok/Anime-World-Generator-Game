# Anime World Generator 1.6 — Character models and expanded reactions

Your generated character now uses a complete full-body illustration with a connected head, neck, outfit and limbs, instead of assembling separate body parts. Each of the nine anime has two male and two female appearance variants, with three victory and three defeat poses: 36 identities and 216 pose illustrations. Saved builds consistently reopen the same identity.

- Added two regular reaction hosts to every anime, bringing the regular cast to 36 speakers.
- Expanded original dialogue for existing and new hosts, including contextual responses and repeat avoidance.
- All 93 battle opponents can react starting with their selection roll. Rerolling replaces the revealed opponent; only the accepted opponent joins subsequent battle reactions.
- Removed the faint player aura oval and added individual silhouette masks to prevent detached pixels or neighboring atlas artwork appearing around the character. The original artwork is preserved.
- Retained saved character data, gems, bonus rerolls, auto-spin and battle rules.
- Updated the app, Windows launcher and offline downloads to version 1.6.

The illustrations represent each anime world and a coherent appearance. Exact age, equipment, abilities and non-human anatomy remain recorded on the character sheet rather than always being drawn literally.

## Downloads

- **Windows.zip:** extract and run `Anime World Generator 1.6.exe`.
- **Browser.zip:** extract the entire archive and open `index.html`; keep all files together.
- **EXE:** standalone Windows launcher containing the complete offline game.
- **SHA256.txt:** checksums for all three downloads.

The Windows launcher extracts to `%LOCALAPPDATA%\AnimeWorldGenerator\1.6`. Saves from the development folder or earlier launcher folders are separate browser storage locations. The launcher is unsigned, as in previous releases.
